# Product Guide: Omlila Gen (Nepali Lyricist & Video Studio)

## 1. Product Vision
Omlila Gen is a specialized AI-powered creative suite designed to generate rich Nepali lyrics and seamlessly orchestrate them into high-quality, 4K lyrical typography videos. It bridges the gap between literary generation and cinematic video production.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Content Creators & Musicians:** Looking to generate authentic Nepali poetry and lyrics.
- **Social Media Influencers:** Needing rapid, aesthetic lyrical videos for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels.
- **Producers:** Seeking an all-in-one studio to sync audio, AI lyrics, and dynamic visual styling.

## 3. Core Capabilities
- **AI Lyric Generation:** Uses advanced, fine-tuned models to generate contextually accurate Nepali lyrics.
- **Interactive Video Studio:** A React-based canvas previewer that supports multi-aspect ratio rendering (9:16, 16:9, 1:1) and real-time playback.
- **Export & Automation:** Exports synchronized lyrical typography directly to MP4 using browser-native muxing.

## 4. Success Metrics
- **Generation Quality:** Producing culturally coherent Nepali lyrics with high user retention.
- **Export Reliability:** Consistent, lag-free 4K video exports.
- **User Engagement:** High interaction rates with the styling, timing, and sequence editors within the studio.
