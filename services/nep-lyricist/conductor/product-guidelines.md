# Product Guidelines: Nepali Lyrics Autocomplete

## 1. UI Design Philosophy
**Minimalist & Clean:** The interface should prioritize function and focus. We will eliminate visual clutter to ensure that the user's primary attention remains entirely on the text input and the generated lyrical continuations.

## 2. Content & Output Handling
**Raw Output:** To maintain the authenticity of the training data and serve as a genuine creative tool, the model will provide raw output. No strict content filtering will be applied to the generated lyrics, allowing the AI to predict naturally based on the input context.

## 3. Language & Script Processing
**Natural Prediction:** The autocomplete feature will not force conversions between Romanized Nepali and Devanagari. Instead, it will rely on natural prediction, outputting the script that the model statistically determines is the most appropriate continuation of the user's input.

## 4. Brand Tone
**Artistic & Expressive:** Despite its minimal interface, the application should feel like a creative companion. The tone of the UI copy, documentation, and overall user experience should be inspiring, tailored toward supporting musicians, songwriters, and creative writers in their artistic process.
